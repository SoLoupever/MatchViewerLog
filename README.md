# MatchViewerLog
MatchViewerLog is a modular pet battle addon for World of Warcraft. Manage pets and teams, import your Rematch data, and install required modules easily. Featuring a modern ViewerLog-style UI, the addon is actively developed 

[![](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1668460/description_1f14e088-f2d7-423b-b41c-ea39a92953a2.png)](https://discord.gg/2gfEKGAT46)

#   
![](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1668460/description_0bdac378-23db-4826-a77d-d6b4e57f04df.png)

**MatchViewerLog** is a World of Warcraft battle pet addon designed to provide a modern, clear, and intuitive way to manage your battle pets, teams, categories, and scripts.

Fully developed independently, MatchViewerLog focuses on **ergonomics, clarity, simplicity, and modularity**, while making it easy for players coming from Rematch to migrate their existing data.

***

## ![](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1668460/description_9d8ac43c-d7d4-4867-bc4a-2d326e02a680.png)

*   🐾 Browse and manage your battle pets
    
*   🔍 Quickly find the pets you need
    
*   🎯 Filter your pets using different criteria
    
*   🧩 Select and configure your pets' abilities
    
*   💬 View detailed information and tooltips for abilities
    
*   🔒 Clearly see which pets you own and which you do not own
    
*   📋 Missing pets remain visible instead of simply appearing as empty slots
    
*   🐾 Easily identify the pets required by your teams

## ![](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1668460/description_bcbde1f5-e7e2-48f0-b8ee-005d7dd8817d.png)

MatchViewerLog provides a complete system for creating and organizing your battle pet teams.

*   📂 Create and organize teams and categories
    
*   ⚔️ Manage your teams directly from the Pet Journal
    
*   👤 Associate teams with specific trainers
    
*   🎯 Quickly find the appropriate team when targeting a trainer
    
*   📥 Import MatchViewerLog teams
    
*   📥 Export MatchViewerLog teams
    
*   📦 Import and export entire categories containing multiple teams
    
*   📥 **Mass import multiple teams at once**
    
*   📤 Export multiple teams and categories
    
*   🧩 Choose whether scripts should be included when importing or exporting teams
    

The **Mass Import** system allows you to import multiple teams in a single operation instead of importing them one by one.

This makes it much easier to restore, transfer, or migrate large collections of battle pet teams.

## ![](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1668460/description_373db7d9-2ed5-443b-8e60-df6e6c2b7fdc.png)

MatchViewerLog includes integrated support for battle pet scripts.

*   📥 Import teams with their scripts
    
*   📥 Import teams without scripts
    
*   📤 Export teams with their scripts
    
*   📤 Export teams without scripts
    
*   📝 Manage scripts directly from MatchViewerLog
    
*   ⌨️ Customize the keyboard shortcut used to interact with scripts
    

The script system is designed to remain simple and accessible while giving advanced users the flexibility they need.

***

## ![](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1668460/description_dd82a933-aed5-4ec3-86dd-0d71fa2bf817.png)

MatchViewerLog includes a **built-in Xu-Fu import system**.

You can import Xu-Fu teams directly from the addon without having to manually convert them through an external website.

The addon includes its own **conversation-based import system**, allowing you to paste your Xu-Fu data directly into MatchViewerLog and have it converted automatically.

### 📦 Mass Import from Xu-Fu

MatchViewerLog also supports **mass importing multiple Xu-Fu teams at once**.

Instead of processing each team individually, you can import multiple teams in a single operation, making large-scale Xu-Fu team imports significantly easier.

No external converter is required.

MatchViewerLog also includes a migration system for players coming from **Rematch**.

You can recover your existing teams and categories without having to recreate everything manually.

The migration system allows you to:

*   📥 Import your existing Rematch teams
    
*   📦 Import multiple teams at once
    
*   📂 Recover your categories
    
*   📜 Recover and convert your scripts
    
*   🔄 Convert your existing data for use with MatchViewerLog
    
*   💾 Keep your existing teams while transitioning to MatchViewerLog
    

The goal is to make the transition as simple and painless as possible.

## ![](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1668460/description_a94b28c7-5100-489f-9a90-c527c7b12ff8.png)

MatchViewerLog includes an XP pet management system that allows you to control which pets should be leveled and in which order.

*   🎲 The **Random XP slot** prioritizes pets from your XP pet list when selecting a pet to level.
    
*   📥 When you **import a team**, any pets that are not yet max level are automatically added to the XP queue.
    
*   🔢 **Choose the XP order** of your pets to decide which pets should be leveled first.
    
*   📋 Easily manage and organize your XP pet list.
    
*   🏷️ Display either the **pet's current name or original name**, even if the pet has been renamed.
    
*   🐾 Pets that cannot battle can automatically be placed at the bottom of the list.
    

This system keeps your **XP queue automatically synchronized with your teams**, while still giving you full control over the order in which your pets are leveled.

## ![](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1668460/description_183d9cec-f93d-421b-adbb-0d11e937a658.png)

MatchViewerLog is built around a **modular architecture**.

The addon is composed of different modules and dependencies, allowing individual features to be developed and maintained independently.

This structure makes it easier to evolve the addon while keeping its core organized, flexible, and stable.

When a feature requires an additional module or dependency, the required components can be installed directly, keeping the installation process as simple as possible.

The goal is to provide the advantages of a modular addon **without making installation complicated for the user**.

![](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1668460/description_65d5fd2a-830d-4931-ba32-5f4338d857aa.png)  
MatchViewerLog intègre un système de gestion des mascottes à XP permettant de contrôler précisément quelles mascottes doivent être mises à niveau et dans quel ordre.

*   🎲 Le **slot Random XP** pioche en priorité une mascotte directement dans votre liste de mascottes à XP.
    
*   📥 Lorsque vous **importez une équipe**, les mascottes qui ne sont pas encore niveau maximum sont automatiquement ajoutées à la file d'XP.
    
*   🔢 **Choisissez l'ordre d'XP** de vos mascottes afin de déterminer lesquelles seront sélectionnées en priorité.
    
*   📋 Gérez et organisez facilement votre liste de mascottes à XP.
    
*   🏷️ Affichez le **nom actuel ou le nom d'origine** de vos mascottes, même lorsqu'elles ont été renommées.
    
*   🐾 Les mascottes qui ne peuvent pas combattre peuvent être automatiquement placées en dernier.
    

Le système permet ainsi de maintenir automatiquement une **file d'XP cohérente avec vos équipes**, tout en vous laissant le contrôle sur l'ordre dans lequel vos mascottes doivent être entraînées.

## 📊 Informations complètes sur les mascottes

MatchViewerLog affiche les informations nécessaires aussi bien pour les mascottes **obtenues que non obtenues**.

Cela permet notamment de savoir rapidement ce qu’une équipe nécessite, quelles mascottes vous possédez déjà et lesquelles vous devez encore obtenir.
